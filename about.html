About VOTEPATH

Our Mission

VOTEPATH is dedicated to bringing transparency and accountability to the electoral process. We track election materials from printing to counting, ensuring that every vote is properly accounted for.

How It Works

Our state-of-the-art tracking system follows ballots and election materials throughout their journey:

  • Printing and production of ballots
  • Secure transportation to polling stations
  • Distribution to voters on election day
  • Collection and counting of completed ballots

Our Team

VOTEPATH was established in 2023 by a team of election security experts, technology specialists, and civic engagement advocates. We believe that transparent elections are essential for a healthy democracy.
